# Business Insights

## Key Findings

### Appointment Performance

* The network recorded **24,000 appointments** with a **76.50% completion rate**.
* **No-shows reached 11.24%**, exceeding cancellations (7.90%) and rescheduling (4.36%), making no-shows the largest source of unsuccessful scheduled appointments.
* **Sunrise Multispecialty Hospital** handled the highest appointment volume (**3,221**) and also had the highest average waiting time (**33.98 minutes**), indicating potential capacity pressure worth further investigation.

### Patient Experience

* **Telemedicine** achieved the highest average satisfaction score (**4.99**), compared with **3.11 for Emergency appointments**.
* The network-wide average waiting time for completed appointments was **29.56 minutes**.

### Hospital Operations

* The network recorded **4,500 admissions** with an average length of stay of **5.41 days**.
* The overall **30-day readmission rate was 3.71%**.
* **Bayou Medical Center** had the highest facility readmission rate at **5.35%**, above the network-wide rate and worth further investigation.

### Financial Performance

* Appointment billing totaled **$3.29M**, while admission charges reached **$51.37M**.
* **Sunrise Multispecialty Hospital** led both appointment billing (**$442K**) and admission charges (**$7.08M**).
* **Pediatrics** had the highest appointment volume, while **Emergency Medicine** generated the highest appointment billing (**$525K**), showing that service volume and financial contribution are not necessarily aligned.

### Trends & Patient Activity

* Monthly appointment volume ranged from **704 to 882**, with the strongest MoM growth at **+10.53%** and the largest decline at **-18.89%**.
* Monthly no-show rates ranged from **8.97% to 13.37%**, showing meaningful variation over time.
* **55.98%** of patients in the appointment-frequency analysis had **6+ appointments**, indicating a substantial group of frequent service users.

## Management Focus

The analysis highlights three areas for deeper investigation: **reducing appointment no-shows, understanding capacity pressure at Sunrise Multispecialty Hospital, and investigating facility-level differences in readmission rates**.
